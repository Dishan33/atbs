Block 4 — Life Span: Adulthood & Ageing (20–65+)

Early Adulthood (20–40)

Cognitive Changes 10M

Schaie's stages: Achieving (applying intelligence to long-term goals) → Responsibility (family, career, community).
Perry's reflective/relativistic thinking: Dualistic → Multiple → Relative subordinate → Full relativism.
Post-formal thought: correct answer varies by situation; emotion influences thinking.
Labouvie-Vief: idealised logic replaced with commitment.

Erikson: Intimacy vs. Isolation 6M

Intimacy requires redefining identity to include another. Cooperative, tolerant, accepting. Too much isolation → competitive, threatened. Successful → generativity.

Levinson's Life Structure 6M

Life structure: underlying design (relationships + occupations). Dream: image of self in adult world. Age-30 transition: reevaluate. Social clock: age-graded expectations. Feminine (marriage/20s) vs. Masculine (career) social clock.

Sternberg's Triangular Theory of Love 6M

3 components: Intimacy (warmth) · Passion (arousal) · Commitment.
Passionate love (beginnings) → Companionate love (intimacy + commitment). Communication requires warmth, forgiveness, sensitivity, acceptance, respect.

Attachment Patterns 3M

Internal working model predicts adult romantic relationships.
Secure (likable, open, trusting) · Avoidant (independent, mistrusting, emotional distance) · Resistant/anxious-ambivalent (fears abandonment, smothering).

Middle Adulthood (40–60) 10M

Erikson: Generativity vs. Stagnation

Generativity = expand influence, commitment to family/society/future generations. Mentoring, volunteering, parenting. Stagnation = self-absorption.

Levinson's Four Tasks

(1) Young-Old: give up youthful qualities. (2) Destruction-Creation: re-evaluate past hurtful acts. (3) Masculinity-Femininity: integrate masculine/feminine aspects. (4) Engagement-Separateness: balance outer engagement with interior needs.

Intelligence

Crystallized (Horn & Hofer, 1992): verbal reasoning, vocabulary — improves through middle age.
Fluid: flexible reasoning, speed — peaks early adulthood, declines. Best works: mathematicians/scientists (20s–30s) vs. historians/philosophers (40s–50s).
Sternberg's triarchic: Analytic (efficient learning) · Creative (flexibility, expertise) · Practical (adapting to context).
Memory/processing decline. Strategies decrease decline.

Relationships

Launching children (no longer negative). U-curve marital satisfaction. Feminisation of poverty. Grandparenthood. Siblings: sister closer. Friends: quality > quantity. Androgyny.

Late Adulthood (65+) 6M

Physical Changes

Primary ageing (biological) vs. Secondary ageing (hereditary/environment).
Hair gray/thin · Skin wrinkled · Presbyopia · Hearing loss (~30% over 60) · Decreased taste/smell/touch.
Young-old vs. Old-old.

Cognitive Changes

Response speeds decline. Working memory decreases.
Selective optimisation with compensation — narrow goals, maximise returns, compensate for losses.
Terminal decline — steady cognitive decrease before death.

Memory

Slower processing · Less use of strategies · Recognition > free recall · Implicit memory more intact than deliberate · Autobiographical memory best recall of adolescence/early adulthood · Prospective memory declines.

Wisdom

Practical knowledge, emotional maturity, listening skills, creativity helping others. Increases with age. Components: knowledge about life, effective strategies, concern for common good, awareness of uncertainties.
Schaie & Willis: decline reversed in 40–60% of elderly with training.

Erikson: Ego Integrity vs. Despair 6M

Integrity = whole, satisfied with life. Despair = bitterness, anxiety about death.

Peck's Three Tasks

(1) Ego differentiation vs. work-role preoccupation — self-worth beyond career.
(2) Body transcendence vs. body preoccupation — value beyond physical.
(3) Ego transcendence vs. ego preoccupation — facing death constructively.

Labouvie-Vief: Emotional Expertise

Elders more in touch with feelings. Better emotional self-regulation. Younger people more impulsively emotional.

Reminiscence & Life Review

Reminiscence: telling stories about past. Life review: reflecting on meaning → greater self-understanding.

Relationships in Late Life

Social convoy: family + friends providing safety/support. Marriage: satisfaction rises if fairness increases. Siblings: sister bonds closer. Friends: offer intimacy, companionship, link to community.

Psychosocial Changes Summary

Secure/multifaceted self-concept · Agreeableness higher · Sociability drops (selective) · Acceptance of change · Spirituality/religiosity advances · Dependency-support vs. independence-ignore scripts.
