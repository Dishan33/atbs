Key Strategic Insights

1. Determinants = MUST master. Asked in EVERY exam. Biological + Psychological combination is the most common 10-mark pattern. Environmental factors appears as 6-mark Section B almost yearly. You cannot pass without knowing all three types of determinants.
2. Six "untouchable" topics = ~45% of all marks. Bandura, Behavioral Assessment, Projective Techniques, Allport, Eysenck, and Determinants appear with the highest frequency across all sections. Master these six first for guaranteed marks.
3. Guilford = Section B guarantee. Appears almost exclusively as 6-mark Section B. Learn it once (7 modalities, 10 traits, SOI model, 3 levels), score 6 marks every exam.
4. Section C (3-mark) pattern. Most repeated: Introverts vs Extroverts (4×), Defense mechanisms (3×), MMPI/NEO-PI (3× each), Ayurvedic body types (3×), Reciprocal determinism (3×), Purpose of assessment (2×).
5. Questions that combine topics. "Define personality and discuss biological factors" (definition + determinants, 3+7). "Define personality and explain FFM" (definition + Big Five, 3+7). "Define behavioral assessment and discuss characteristics" (definition + characteristics).
6. Changing trends. Rorschach less frequent recently. Ayurvedic body types emerged post-2015. Eysenck vs Guilford comparison was old format. NEO-PI is a consistent 3-mark favorite.
7. The 10+6+3 strategy. Each topic can appear at any mark level. Prepare each major topic with: 10-mark version (detailed theory), 6-mark version (key points), 3-mark version (concise definition/short note).
