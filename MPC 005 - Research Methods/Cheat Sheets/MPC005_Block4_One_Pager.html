Qualitative research

One-page revision sheet

Qualitative approach

Studies meaning, experience and context through non-numerical evidence and interpretive analysis.

Contrast with quantitative work: numerical measurement, statistical analysis and tests of relationships/differences.

Common approaches: case study, ethnography, phenomenology, grounded theory and discourse analysis.

Ethnography

Studies culture or community in its natural setting.

  1. Gain access and build rapport.
  2. Observe, participate and record field notes.
  3. Use interviews/documents; analyse patterns.
  4. Report with ethics and reflexivity.

Grounded theory

Generates theory from data through constant comparison.

Open coding: concepts. Axial coding: relationships among categories. Selective coding: integrate around a core category.

Use theoretical sampling and memo writing to refine categories.

Discourse, analysis and report

Discourse analysis: examines how talk/text constructs meaning, identity, action and power.

Analyse by familiarising, coding patterns, interpreting context and supporting claims with extracts.

Evaluate credibility, dependability, confirmability and transferability. Report method, researcher position, evidence, findings and limits.

PYQ answer frame: Define → explain assumptions and steps → add an appropriate example → show ethics/reflexivity or quality criteria. Grounded theory and discourse analysis recur across mark levels.
