Research designs

One-page revision sheet

Research design and variance

A research design is the plan, structure and strategy for answering a question and controlling variance.

MAXMINCON: maximise systematic variance; minimise error variance; control extraneous variance.

Single-factor design

One IV with two or more levels.

Between-group: different participants per condition; no carryover.

Within-subject: same participants in all conditions; controls individual differences. Use counterbalancing for order, practice and fatigue effects.

Factorial design

Two or more factors with levels. A 2 × 2 design has four cells.

Main effect: overall effect of one factor.

Interaction: one factor's effect changes across levels of another factor.

Use a small example to show both factors and the DV.

Quasi, correlation and comparison

Quasi-experiment: intervention without full random assignment. Types: one-group pretest-posttest, non-equivalent control group, interrupted time series.

Correlation: association between measured variables; prediction only, no causal proof.

Comparative: compares pre-existing groups; alternative explanations remain.

PYQ answer frame: Define the design → explain its structure → use an example → state control/validity implications. Factorial and quasi-experimental designs need long-answer preparation.
