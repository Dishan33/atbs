PYQ priority map · 2011–2025

Examiners ask for precise method.

Start with repeat clusters. For a long answer, write the definition, organise the explanation, use an example and close by answering the command word.

ESSENTIAL

Research foundations

Research process, good research, reliability, validity, variables, constructs, hypothesis and sampling.

  • Reliability appears in 10- and 6-mark questions
  • Validity threats need a precise comparison
ESSENTIAL

Survey & non-experiment

Survey steps and data collection, ex-post facto, field experiment, case study and correlation.

  • Steps + types is a frequent answer shape
  • Use a clear contrast when prompted
ESSENTIAL

Research designs

Single-factor, factorial, quasi-experimental and comparative designs appear in long answers.

  • Know main vs interaction effect
  • Pair each design with a small example
ESSENTIAL

Qualitative research

Ethnography, grounded theory, discourse analysis, analysis and reporting appear at each mark level.

  • Grounded theory and discourse analysis recur most
  • Steps and assumptions matter as much as definitions
