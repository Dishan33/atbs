Key Strategic Insights

1. Use heat for revision order, not for answer content. Low-frequency topics still appear as 10-mark questions, so revise their linked framework after securing the very-high and high groups.
2. Treat the paper as a design continuum. Distinguish true experiment, field experiment, quasi-experiment, ex-post facto, causal-comparative and correlational designs by manipulation, random assignment, control and the strength of causal inference.
3. Design questions reward visible structure. Start with the definition, then give features/types or steps, a compact psychology example, advantages/limitations where asked, and a conclusion matched to the design’s control.
4. Qualitative questions are procedural. State the approach and its assumptions, then show fieldwork/data generation, analysis, reflexivity/ethics and quality criteria. Do not replace the procedure with generic “advantages”.
