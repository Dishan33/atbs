Key Strategic Insights

1. Method words control the answer. “Explain steps”, “differentiate”, “evaluate” and “describe types” need visibly different structures.
2. Never overclaim. Correlation does not establish causation, and a quasi-experiment does not provide the same control as randomised experimental design.
3. Qualitative answers are procedural. State the assumptions, then fieldwork/coding/interpretation steps, ethics and quality safeguards.
4. Use the 10 + 6 + 3 strategy. Major topics require a full structure, a compressed point-form answer and an exact definition/distinction.
