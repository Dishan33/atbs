MPC-006 concept map

Statistics as a chain of decisions.

Start with the measurement scale, study design and distribution. Those three choices determine whether the result is described, tested, related, predicted or compared through a parametric or non-parametric route.

Central question

What does this data allow us to conclude?

Describe data

  • Classification, tabulation and frequency distributions
  • Central tendency and dispersion
  • Levels of measurement and graphs
  • Descriptive versus inferential statistics

Test a claim

  • Null and alternative hypotheses
  • Level of significance and confidence limits
  • Type I and Type II errors
  • Standard error and t for mean differences

Relate & predict

  • Pearson r, scatterplots and assumptions
  • Rank and special correlations
  • Partial, part and multiple correlation
  • Regression equation and prediction

Compare groups

  • Normal distribution, skewness and kurtosis
  • One-way and two-way ANOVA
  • Interaction effect and degrees of freedom
  • Mann-Whitney, Wilcoxon, Kruskal-Wallis and chi-square
