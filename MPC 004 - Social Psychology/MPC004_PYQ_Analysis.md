# MPC-004 Advanced Social Psychology - PYQ Analysis (2011-2025)

Source: `PYQ_MPC-004_2011-2025.pdf` (69 pages; papers through December 2025).

## What the papers reward

The corpus returns to the same concepts at different lengths:

1. **10 marks:** define the concept, organise its components or theory, add an illustration, study, application or evaluation, and conclude.
2. **6 marks:** definition plus a structured list of factors, stages, methods, strategies or distinctions.
3. **3 marks:** a precise definition, two key features or a distinction, and a named theorist or study where it adds precision.

## Mark-specific answer moves

### 10 marks

Use: **definition -> conceptual map -> named theory or study -> illustration/application -> brief evaluation/conclusion**.

If the question has a split, use that split as visible headings. For a `3+7` question, spend roughly one short paragraph on the first demand and the body of the answer on the second.

### 6 marks

Use: **definition -> 4-6 labelled points -> one named study/example -> one-line close**.

For methods, the labelled points should normally include procedure plus a strength/limitation or validity issue. For theory, state the mechanism rather than just listing the theorist.

### 3 marks

Use: **one exact definition -> two features or one distinction -> named theorist/study if useful**.

Prepare especially: door-in-the-face, low-ball, normative social influence, social impact, schema, prototype, cognitive algebra, primacy-recency, halo effect, active/inactive crowd, cohesion, social identity, informed consent, debriefing and the correlational method.

## Comparisons that require separate definitions

- Conformity vs compliance vs obedience
- Stereotype vs prejudice vs discrimination
- Attitude vs belief
- Observation vs correlation vs experiment
- Crowd behaviour vs collective behaviour
- Cooperation vs competition

## Named anchors worth memorising

- Asch - conformity
- Cialdini - compliance principles and strategies
- Heider - balance theory and attribution
- Kelley - covariation principle
- Katz - functional theory of attitudes
- Bem - self-perception theory
- Kelman - three-process theory of attitude organisation
- Milgram, Stanford Prison and Hofling - obedience discussion
- Darley and Latane - helping/bystander discussion
- Batson - empathy-altruism hypothesis
- Tuckman - group development
- Tajfel and Turner - social identity theory

## Guardrail

The PYQs identify exam emphasis; they do not replace the prescribed blocks. Keep course terminology and any detailed explanation traceable to the prescribed materials, and use a study or theorist only when it serves the question being asked.
