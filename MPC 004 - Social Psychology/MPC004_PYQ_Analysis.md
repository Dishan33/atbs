# MPC-004 Advanced Social Psychology - PYQ Analysis (2011-2025)

Source: `PYQ_MPC-004_2011-2025.pdf` (69 pages; papers through December 2025).

## What the papers reward

The paper repeatedly tests a small set of concepts in three forms:

1. 10-mark essays: define the concept, explain its components/theories, then add an illustration, study, application, or evaluation.
2. 6-mark answers: definition plus a structured list of factors, stages, methods, or strategies.
3. 3-mark notes: crisp definitions, distinctions, named theories, and landmark experiments.

## High-return answer frames

### 10 marks

Use: **definition -> conceptual map -> theory/study -> Indian or everyday illustration -> brief evaluation/conclusion**.

### 6 marks

Use: **definition -> 4-6 labelled points -> one named study/example -> one-line conclusion**.

### 3 marks

Use: **one precise definition -> 2 key features or a distinction -> named theorist/study where relevant**.

## PYQ patterns to prepare first

- Nature/concept/scope/history/applications of social psychology.
- Experimental, correlational, observational and ethnographic methods; ethics and threats to validity.
- Conformity, compliance and obedience; Asch and Cialdini are repeat anchors.
- Attitude formation/change and attitude-organisation theories.
- Prejudice reduction; stereotypes, prejudice and discrimination compared.
- Aggression causes, theories and reduction.
- Prosocial behaviour, altruism and cooperation.
- Person perception, attribution errors, schemas and interpersonal attraction.
- Group definition, characteristics, types, formation, dynamics and development.
- Crowd behaviour and social conflict.

## Named anchors worth memorising

- Asch - conformity
- Cialdini - compliance principles and strategies
- Heider - balance theory and attribution
- Katz - functional theory of attitudes
- Bem - self-perception theory
- Milgram, Stanford Prison, Hofling - obedience discussion
- Darley and Latane - helping/bystander discussion
- Batson - empathy-altruism hypothesis
- Tuckman - group development
- Tajfel and Turner - social identity theory

## Guardrail

The PYQs identify exam emphasis; they do not replace the prescribed blocks. Every answer bank entry should remain traceable to the block PDFs and should distinguish evidence-based claims from examples.
