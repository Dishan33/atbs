PYQ corpus - 2011 to 2025

Let the papers set the priority.

A question-pattern map from all 69 pages of the MPC-004 corpus. It records what has actually appeared across the papers; use the prescribed blocks for the full academic explanation.

MPC-004 repeatedly tests the same concepts at different lengths. A long answer asks for a theory or structure; a six-marker narrows to factors, stages or methods; a three-marker tests a clean definition, distinction or named term. Revise by cluster, then rehearse the mark-specific move.

ESSENTIAL

Foundations & methods

Social psychology's nature, history, applications, research methods, validity and ethics recur through the corpus.

  • Experimental, quasi-experimental, correlational, observational and ethnographic methods
  • Control, validity, consent and debriefing
ESSENTIAL

Influence & attitudes

Conformity, compliance, obedience, attitude formation/change and organisation theories appear in every mark band.

  • Asch, Cialdini, Heider, Katz, Bem and Kelman
  • Never blur conformity, compliance and obedience
ESSENTIAL

Prejudice & group life

Stereotypes, prejudice, discrimination, group characteristics, formation and dynamics are persistent long-answer territory.

  • Development, maintenance, manifestation and reduction
  • Rules, cohesion, communication and social identity
HIGH

Social cognition & action

Person perception, attribution, attraction, helping, aggression, conflict and crowd behaviour cycle across papers.

  • Schemas, prototypes, cognitive algebra and attribution errors
  • Theory plus an applied implication is the usual demand

What to drill next

3-mark precision

Terms that need a clean 100-word note

  • Door-in-the-face and low-ball techniques
  • Normative social influence and social impact
  • Schema, prototype, cognitive algebra, primacy-recency and halo effect
  • Active/inactive crowd, group cohesion and social identity
  • Informed consent, debriefing and correlational method

Comparison traps

Pairs that examiners separate

  • Conformity vs compliance vs obedience
  • Stereotype vs prejudice vs discrimination
  • Attitude vs belief
  • Observation vs correlation vs experiment
  • Crowd behaviour vs collective behaviour; cooperation vs competition

Split-mark questions

Follow the marks visibly

  • Use the split in headings: for example, concept 3 + factors 7.
  • Give the definition only once, then spend most space on the higher-mark demand.
  • For theory questions, name the theory, mechanism, supporting illustration and one limitation or application.
  • For methods, include procedure plus merit/demerit or validity issue when asked.

The answer frame to rehearse

10 marks: definition - conceptual map - named theory/study - illustration or application - brief evaluation/conclusion. 6 marks: definition - 4 to 6 labelled points - one named anchor. 3 marks: exact definition - two features or one distinction - theorist/study only where it adds precision.
