PYQ corpus · 2011–2025

Let the papers set the priority.

A question-pattern map from the 69-page MPC‑004 PYQ corpus. It identifies recurring answer demands; it does not replace the prescribed block reading.

The paper repeatedly returns to durable distinctions, classic studies, research methods and applications. Prepare the essential clusters first, then use 6- and 3-mark items to sharpen definitions, theorists and comparisons.

ESSENTIAL

Foundations & method

Nature, scope, history, application and research ethics recur in long and medium answers.

  • Experiment, correlation, observation, ethnography and quasi-experiment
  • Validity, informed consent and debriefing
ESSENTIAL

Influence & attitudes

Conformity, compliance, obedience, formation/change and organisation theories are repeated anchors.

  • Asch, Cialdini, informational vs normative influence
  • Heider, Katz and Bem
ESSENTIAL

Prejudice & group life

Stereotypes, prejudice, discrimination, group characteristics, formation and dynamics appear across mark bands.

  • Prejudice reduction is a repeat favourite
  • Group identity and crowd behaviour also recur
HIGH

Helping, aggression & conflict

Prosocial behaviour, attraction, aggression and conflict reward theory plus an applied resolution/reduction section.

  • Empathy-altruism and cooperation
  • Frustration-aggression and social learning

Groups, dynamics and crowd behaviour10, 6, 3Define first, organise through features/stages/theories, and distinguish group, crowd and collective behaviour.

The answer frame to rehearse

10 marks: definition → conceptual map → named theory/study → example or application → evaluation/conclusion. 6 marks: definition → 4–6 labelled points → one named anchor. 3 marks: exact definition → two features or one distinction → theorist/study where useful.
