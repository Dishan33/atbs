Key Strategic Insights

1. Prepare the distinction before the theory. The corpus repeatedly separates conformity/compliance/obedience, stereotype/prejudice/discrimination, attitude/belief, and observation/correlation/experiment.
2. Methods are a core scoring route. A strong method answer gives definition, procedure, strength, limitation and the relevant validity or ethics issue.
3. Group life is not one topic. Group definition, rules, types, formation, development, communication and cohesion are separately examinable prompts.
4. Use the 10 + 6 + 3 strategy. Keep a long explanation, a six-point version and a crisp short note for every essential cluster.
