Four-block cheat sheets

Keep the structure in view.

Use these as your orientation layer before going back to the prescribed units for details and examples.

Block 1 · foundations & perception

How social psychology explains people in context

  • Nature, scope, history, levels of analysis and applications
  • Methods: experiment, correlation, observation, ethnography and quasi-experiment
  • Ethics, validity and data collection
  • Person perception: schemas, prototypes, impression formation, attribution and cognitive algebra

Block 2 · influence & interaction

How people alter one another

  • Conformity, compliance and obedience; Asch and Cialdini
  • Prosocial behaviour, altruism and cooperation
  • Interpersonal attraction and relationship processes
  • Aggression, conflict, theories, causes and reduction

Block 3 · attitudes & prejudice

How evaluations become social patterns

  • Nature, components, organisation, formation and change of attitudes
  • Heider's balance theory, Katz's functional theory and Bem's self-perception theory
  • Stereotypes, prejudice, discrimination and their distinctions
  • Development, manifestation and reduction of prejudice

Block 4 · groups & collective life

How groups organise social behaviour

  • Group definition, features, types, structure and formation theories
  • Group dynamics, development, cohesion, communication and conflict
  • Social identity and group behaviour
  • Crowd and collective behaviour
